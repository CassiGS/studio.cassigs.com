## Adding some pep

I've been following along with [Lisa Bardot's](https://bardotbrush.com/about-lisa-bardot/) Skillshare class, [Kickstart your Creativity with Procreate](https://www.skillshare.com/en/classes/kickstart-your-creativity-with-procreate-20-fun-drawings-for-beginners-and-beyond/1537565404) and in it we drew a pizza with in sort of a cartoonish style. For whatever reason that inspired my brain to think of this phrase and the cartoon style had me thinking of the rubber hose style. Now this style might be a little played out, but I did have a lot of fun drawing this little peppy pepperoni nonetheless.
